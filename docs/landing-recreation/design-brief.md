# Landing recreation brief

Recreate Neeraj Butola’s portfolio landing page as one spacious editorial composition: the feeling of opening an engineer’s personal notebook. A compact masthead gives way to a generous, full-width hero, an unbroken Newsreader headline and the approved workbench illustration. Keep the familiar cream, forest and teal palette; orange remains a restrained action accent. The page should feel personal and considered, with less competing text and enough space to understand each idea before the next.

Let the typography carry the narrative: Newsreader for the headline and section titles, DM Sans for navigation, descriptions and controls. Preserve the exact approved hero words and its single Blog action. Progress from projects worth inspecting, through a clear view of the technical toolkit and real career history, into a simple invitation to connect. A single quiet arrival animation introduces the hero; keyboard focus and reduced-motion preferences remain first-class. About stays the final main content section before the newsletter/footer.

Take inspiration from the measured pace of an independent publication and the clarity of a working studio: the original interactive project selector beside one large preview, tools arranged by purpose, and company marks placed directly on a career timeline. Use layout and scale for distinction, rather than repeated boxes, ornamental rules, fabricated statistics or extra marketing copy. Keep every real link, all nineteen technology icons including Python, circular company logos, newsletter and RSS. Inner pages retain their current shell.
